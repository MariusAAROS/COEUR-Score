# STATUS

## Badge applied for

**Artifacts Available**

## Justification

We are applying for the **Artifacts Available** badge.

The COEUR-Score artifact has been placed on a publicly accessible archival
repository and is permanently retrievable:

- The complete source code, datasets, experiment notebooks, and the packaging
  files required to build and run the artifact are publicly hosted.
- The artifact is released under the license included in the repository
  ([`LICENSE`](LICENSE)), permitting reviewers and future researchers to access
  and use it.
- To support long-term availability and citation, the artifact is (or will be)
  deposited on an archival service that issues a persistent identifier (DOI).

The repository is self-contained and includes everything needed to obtain and
inspect the artifact:

- the COEUR-Score implementation (`coeur/`) and baselines (`coeur/baseline/`);
- the datasets used in the paper (`datasets/`);
- the experiment and demonstration notebooks (`experiments/`, `example/`);
- machine-readable packaging descriptors for reproducible execution —
  [`Dockerfile`](Dockerfile), [`docker-compose.yml`](docker-compose.yml), and
  the pinned [`requirements-linux.txt`](requirements-linux.txt);
- documentation describing how to obtain, build, and run the artifact
  ([`README.md`](README.md) and [`REQUIREMENTS.md`](REQUIREMENTS.md)).

Because the artifact is publicly available, permanently archived under a stable
identifier, and accompanied by the files needed to retrieve and use it, we
believe it satisfies the criteria for the **Artifacts Available** badge.
